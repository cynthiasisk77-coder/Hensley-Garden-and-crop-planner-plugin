# Hensley Garden & Crop Planner for Claude

Garden research from the Hensley Garden & Crop Planner app, right in Claude: crop guides
built from university Extension sources and 40 years of East Texas growing by the Hensley
family.

## What it does

- **Frost dates** for any US ZIP code (NOAA 1991–2020 climate normals), with the length of
  the frost-free season.
- **Plant & Soil Matchmaker:** tell it your ZIP and, if you know them, your sun, soil pH,
  soil type and whether you grow in the ground, a raised bed or containers. It checks over
  150 vegetables, herbs and fruits against your frost dates, season length, rainfall, summer
  heat and USDA zone, and lists the best fits with a reason for each.
- **Crop facts:** seed depth, spacing, sun, water, soil pH, days to harvest and when to plant,
  each with its Extension source, plus a Hensley family tip when there is one.
- **Garden topics:** short summaries of research articles on soil, mulch, watering, seeds,
  feeding and regional growing, with links to the sources.
- **Pest and disease identification:** likely matches for what you see, with the signs to
  look for and common look-alikes.

It is read-only. There is no sign-in, and it does not store your questions or build a
profile of you. It does not give canning or pesticide instructions.

## Try it

After installing, ask Claude things like:

```text
When is the last frost for ZIP 75701?
What should I plant in my raised bed? ZIP 75701, full sun, soil pH 6.2.
How far apart do I plant okra?
Something is putting holes in my squash leaves. What could it be?
```

## Install

In Claude, open the plugin directory, search for **Hensley Garden**, and select **Install**.
In Claude Code, run `/plugin`, find Hensley Garden & Crop Planner, and install it.
